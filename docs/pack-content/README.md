# Regional dialogue and playlist preparation

These files are original draft scripts for recording, not recordings or licensed film clips. Review phrasing with a local speaker. Regional scripts must be recorded by someone who agrees to their distribution; unsupported dialects must not be pasted into the text-to-speech importer.

Keep the first packs friendly and suitable for calls: greetings, missed-mute jokes, chai breaks, lag, deadlines and friendly reactions. Avoid regional stereotypes and jokes about protected identities.

Record mono WAV masters, trim silence and normalize listening volume. Prepare short compressed copies within the upload limits shown by the admin. Keep a rights record naming the writer, performer, permission, date and attribution. Use Free for third-party YouTube playlists. Original hosted audio stays disabled until the owner reviews R2 billing; the bundled original Phonk loops work without R2.

For NCS playlists, choose specific official uploads, verify embedding works and follow each track's current NCS usage/credit rules. The NCS name alone does not establish unrestricted licensing. Save `URL | title | artist` and the required credit. No unverified YouTube IDs are seeded into the app.

Nothing in this directory has been uploaded or published.

Video meme packs use the same provider-hosted model as the Phonk catalog. The admin stores an official YouTube watch, Shorts or youtu.be link plus title and creator; the public room displays YouTube's visible embed. The five default folders are Worldwide Classics, Worldwide Trending, India & Regional, Gaming and After Dark. Users cannot modify these shared folders, and India is a discovery category rather than a country lock. This avoids R2 storage for videos, but embedding and credit do not create reuse rights: verify the upload and its permissions before marking it official.

For owner-supplied Indian selections, new links are clean by default. Move an item to After Dark and mark it 18+ only when the owner explicitly says it is 18+.
