# Global meme pack expansion candidates

These are additional globally recognizable audio-meme candidates for research and later audio upload. They are grouped by how users should discover them. Every item remains Draft until the exact original source and redistribution licence are recorded.

## Visibility model

| Pack | Region setting | Audience | Default visibility |
|---|---|---|---|
| Universal Reactions & SFX | For everyone (`common`) | Works without understanding a language | Visible to everyone |
| Global Internet Classics | Worldwide (`global`) | Mostly English internet culture used internationally | Visible to everyone; English label |
| Global Gaming Memes | Worldwide (`global`) | International gaming audiences | Visible to everyone |
| Global Trending | Worldwide (`global`) | Rotating current memes | Visible to everyone; reviewed regularly |
| Global After Dark | Worldwide (`global`) | Strong language/adult humour | Hidden until adult-content opt-in |

Region is a discovery label, not a country lock. Users may browse every public pack.

## Global After Dark

- Keep strong-language clips in a separate pack with `explicit=true`.
- Do not include racial slurs, targeted harassment or sexual clips involving unclear consent.
- An adult toggle controls visibility; it does not make unlicensed audio publishable.
- Each candidate still needs the same Credit, Source and Licence fields as a clean meme.
