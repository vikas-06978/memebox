# Release preparation, not publication

No deployment or store upload is authorized yet. Keep the version at 1.3.0 until the owner chooses the release version.

## Draft store summary

Make calls more playful with MemeBox: meme sounds, original music, camera lights and animated characters for supported browser calls.

Free includes the soundboard, offline voices, Cat and Dog, a still lamp, three filters and two tunes. Pro time passes add lights, voice effects, camera captions, Follow me, six characters, eight filters and seven tunes, plus original Desi Phonk. Pro Max adds all 14 characters, 20 filters, 13 tunes and all three original Phonk loops. Purchased pass time has no calendar expiry and runs while enabled in a supported call.

Eight optional characters can be bought permanently. Unicorn, Robot, Snake and Bird are Pro Max exclusives for new purchases. Existing unlimited keys and previously purchased character entitlements remain supported.

Choose from 12 complete interface languages and four marked Beta choices: German, Italian, Japanese and Punjabi. Advanced Beta text currently falls back to English. Offline speech supports 26 languages; regional dialect recordings are a separate content collection, still in preparation.

Original Phonk loops synthesize on your device without hosted audio files or ads. Free third-party playlists use a visible official YouTube player, which may show YouTube ads. Your own picture memes can stay on the outgoing camera until you close them.

Do not claim real international checkout, live settlement readiness, fully translated Beta bundles, zero infrastructure costs at unlimited traffic, or real Meet performance before those checks are complete.
