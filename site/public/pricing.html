How the hours work

  1. Buy hours. They never expire.
  2. Switch your pass on in the 😂 panel when you want it.
  3. The clock only runs while you are in a call with your pass on. Pause any time.
  4. When the hours are used up, the extras switch off. Everything free stays free.

Try before you pay
