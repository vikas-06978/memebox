MemeBox

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

The short version

  • MemeBox does not upload your live microphone or camera feed to MemeBox servers. Recording or importing a personal clip saves it on your device.
  • MemeBox does not add analytics or advertisements. Optional YouTube players use YouTube’s services and may show ads or process data under YouTube’s privacy policy.
  • Your lines, clips and settings stay in your own browser.
  • The feedback form stores only what you type, plus the MemeBox version.

Your microphone, tab audio and camera

On a supported call website, MemeBox mixes your real microphone with the meme audio you choose, inside your browser tab. The call website sends that mix to the call as it would send your normal microphone. The voice changer and the optional camera captions also work only inside your browser tab. When you send another tab's sound, only the tab you picked is mixed in, only after you click, and only until you stop. MemeBox processes sound levels and effects locally; it does not transcribe your conversation or upload your live feed to its servers. A recording you deliberately create in the clip editor stays on your device.

Lights & looks (an option, off until you switch it on) draws lights, filters, characters and music onto your own camera picture and microphone inside your browser tab. With "Follow me" switched on, MemeBox also looks at a small copy of your camera picture, about 10 times a second, and runs small face and hand models that are bundled inside the extension, to know where your face is (left, right, standing up, out of the picture), how it looks (smile, blink, tongue out) and whether a hand is raised (a wave, thumbs up). This happens only on your computer, with no download and no internet: the picture is never saved, recorded or sent anywhere, and nothing is learned about who you are.

Data on your device

Your lines, imported clips, personal music, pictures and settings are stored on your device. Playing them into a call sends the selected audio or picture through the call service; exporting shares the file you choose. They are not automatically published to other MemeBox users. Uninstalling the extension removes its saved extension data. Website preferences remain in that website’s browser storage until you clear them.

Music room and YouTube

Original tunes are synthesized on your device. Private playlist labels, links, language choices and self-declared strong-language preferences are saved in your browser. The website player receives the selected playlist or video reference when you choose to play; private labels are passed in the URL fragment, which is not sent to the web server. They are not published in the shared catalog.

YouTube is contacted when you select its player. It receives information needed to deliver the video, including your network address and browser information, and may use ads, cookies or other identifiers under its own policy. MemeBox uses the visible privacy-enhanced YouTube player; it does not remove ads, download its audio or bypass age/region restrictions. Sending the player tab into a call also sends any ad audio.

Browsing published MemeBox collections requests the owner-managed catalog from our site. Personal collections stay separate. Strong-language markers and the 18+ preference are self-declared; they are not verified age checks or automatic classification of unknown videos.

Feedback form and uninstall survey

The extension opens our feedback page with only the MemeBox version and the call site in the link, for example ?v=1.0.0&site=meet. When you uninstall, a one-question survey opens. Answering is optional.

If you send a form, we store what you picked or typed (rating, features used, ideas, message, where you used MemeBox and which device, both picked from a list, and your email only if you type one), the version, the call site, the page language and the time. We don't store your IP address or browser details. To stop spam, we count messages per hour using a one-way hash of your IP address and delete that count after the hour. The form uses Cloudflare Turnstile as a spam check. Answers are kept in a Cloudflare D1 database that only the developer can read.

Buying Pro and license keys

If you buy something on the /buy page, we store your product, amount, currency, order time, order status and the license key it gives. Manual UPI orders also store the transaction ID (UTR) you submit. Gateway orders store payment and refund identifiers and a hashed recovery token. Your browser keeps the recovery token so you can return to your order. Clearing browser storage removes that recovery shortcut.

Manual payments happen in your UPI app. When Razorpay checkout is enabled, its payment interface processes the payment under Razorpay's privacy policy. MemeBox does not collect your card number, CVV, bank password or UPI PIN. The current gateway integration is test-only and does not collect real money.

For time passes, we store signed usage-session records and cumulative reported seconds to reconcile retries. We do not store call recordings, meeting conversations or camera footage.

If you enter a license key in MemeBox, the extension sends only that key to our site, to check it (about once a day). We store the day of the last check. Saved personal media is not uploaded to MemeBox servers; you choose what to send through your call or export.
