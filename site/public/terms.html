MemeBox is a free browser extension that plays sounds into your own microphone during video calls. By installing or using it you agree to these terms.

Your content

Pictures

Picture memes use pictures you add yourself. Only use pictures you are allowed to use. Don't use photos of other people without their permission, and never to embarrass or bully anyone.

A key may be turned off if it is shared publicly, or if a payment is reversed. If something goes wrong with a payment, write to us with your order code and we will fix it or refund you.

Tab audio

"Send this tab's sound" plays another tab's audio live into your call. It doesn't download anything. Respect the rights of the people who made that content.
