How to play meme sounds in Discord

  1. Add MemeBox

    Install the free MemeBox extension for Chrome or Edge.

  2. Join your Discord call

    Open discord.com in Chrome or Edge and join a voice channel. The 😂 button appears in the corner.

  3. Click 😂

    A meme plays through your mic and everyone hears it. Right-click 😂 to pick a sound, search, or change your voice.

Questions about MemeBox and Discord

Does everyone in the Discord call hear the memes?

Yes. MemeBox plays the meme through your microphone, so everyone in the Discord call hears it, just like your voice. They don't need MemeBox.

Is it free to use MemeBox with Discord?

Yes. The soundboard, Hindi and English voices, six tones and tab audio are free. Pro (₹99 once) adds unlimited picture memes, the voice changer and more.

Is my voice recorded?

Never. MemeBox only mixes sound inside your browser tab. Nothing is saved or uploaded.

Does it work in the Discord desktop app?

MemeBox works in the browser version of Discord (Chrome or Edge). No bot and no server permissions needed. The sound goes through your own mic.
