Questions

Do the others in the call need MemeBox?

No. The meme goes out through your microphone, so they hear it like your voice.

Does it work in the Zoom or Teams desktop app?

No, only in the browser versions. Open your meeting link in Chrome or Edge.

Why can't anyone hear the memes?

Check that you're not muted in the call. Memes are silent while you're muted. If you just installed or updated MemeBox, reload the call tab once.

How do I pay for the extras?

Buy hours on the pricing page. In India pay with any UPI app (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM); card payment in dollars is coming soon. Your key appears once the payment is confirmed.

Is my voice recorded?

Never. MemeBox only mixes sound inside your browser tab. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.
