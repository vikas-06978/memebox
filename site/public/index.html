Meme sounds and a voice changer for your calls

Click 😂 and a meme plays through your microphone, so everyone in the call hears it. Only you need MemeBox.

Add to Chrome, it's free See how it works

Works in the browser on Google Meet, Zoom, Microsoft Teams and Discord. Also in Microsoft Edge.

What you can do

😂 One-click memes

A random line on click, or pick from the panel. Alt+1 to Alt+9 for your favourites.

🗣️ Hindi and English voices

Six tones from Chipmunk to Villain. The speech engine runs on your own computer.

🎤 Voice changer

Chipmunk, Deep, Robot, Echo or Radio for your own voice. Alt+V switches it on and off.

📺 Any tab into the call

A funny YouTube moment? Send that tab's sound into the call. Nothing is downloaded.

🎵 Your own clips

Upload audio or video, record yourself, trim it, and play it with one key.

📦 Packs

College, Cricket, Office and Party packs with friendly roasts. Share your own as a file.

How it works

  1. 1. Add MemeBox

    Install it from the store. A short welcome page shows the basics and tests your mic.

  2. 2. Join your call

    Open Meet, Zoom, Teams or Discord in the browser. The 😂 button appears in the corner.

  3. 3. Click 😂

    The meme goes into your mic. Everyone hears it, and a big caption shows on your screen.

Private by design

Your microphone, camera and tab audio are only mixed inside your browser. Nothing is recorded or uploaded. No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Read the privacy policy.

Questions

Do the others in the call need MemeBox?

No. The meme goes out through your microphone, so they hear it like your voice.

Does it work in the Zoom or Teams desktop app?

No, only in the browser versions. Open your meeting link in Chrome or Edge.

Why can't anyone hear the memes?

Check that you're not muted in the call. Memes are silent while you're muted. If you just installed or updated MemeBox, reload the call tab once.

Is it free?
