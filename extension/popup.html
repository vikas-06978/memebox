How does this work?
  1. Join your call in another tab (Meet, Zoom web, Teams web or Discord web).
  2. In this tab, open the YouTube video or Instagram reel and start it at the funny moment.
  3. Click Send this tab's sound. Everyone in the call hears it through your mic.
  4. Click ⏹ when you're done, or use ⏹ in the 😂 panel.

Nothing is downloaded or recorded. The sound is only mixed live into your mic.
