Welcome to MemeBox 😂

Three steps and you're the funniest person in the call.

  1. 1 Join your call in the browser

    Google Meet, Zoom (web), Microsoft Teams (web) or Discord (web). The 😂 button appears in the corner. If the call was already open, reload it once.

  2. 2 Click 😂 to play a meme

    It goes into your microphone, so everyone in the call hears it. They don't need MemeBox. When you're muted, memes are silent too.

  3. 3 Right-click 😂 for everything else

    Pick any line, search, change your voice (Alt+V), or play favourites with Alt+1 to Alt+9. To play a YouTube video into the call, click the MemeBox icon in the toolbar on that tab.

Mic test

Check that your microphone works. The sound stays on this page. Nothing is recorded.

You're ready. Your lines, clips and packs are in Settings.
