Shortcuts Alt+1 … Alt+4 work right away. For Alt+5 … Alt+9 (or to change keys) open chrome://extensions/shortcuts. While the call page has focus, Alt+5…9 also work without setting them there.

Add a meme from a link

Nothing is downloaded or saved: the sound is fetched from the link each time you play it (so it needs internet), and Chrome asks you once per website. YouTube / Instagram videos: open the video in a tab and click the MemeBox toolbar icon → Send this tab's sound into my call.

Timed lines

Off by default. Timers only fire while a call tab is open and your mic is connected.

    Backup

    Export includes all lines, timers and uploaded clips. Import adds to your lines; lines with the same id are replaced.

    Privacy

    Your microphone audio is only mixed inside this browser tab. Nothing is recorded, saved or sent anywhere. There are no accounts, servers or analytics.
