Lines

LineAboutShortcutActions

Alt+1…3 and Alt+0 work everywhere. Change keys or add Alt+4…9 at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.

Add a clip

Use an audio file or a video (only its sound is kept), a direct file link, or record yourself. Then trim it. A saved clip can be up to 1 MB.

YouTube, Instagram and other video pages are never downloaded. Play them live with the toolbar button → “Send this tab's sound”.

Packs

    Share packs

      A pack holds lines and their clips. Importing adds to your lines, and lines with the same id are replaced. Files are checked strictly.

      Timed lines / Party mode

      Off by default. Timers only fire while a call tab is open and your mic is connected.

        Privacy

        Your microphone and tab audio are only mixed inside this browser. Nothing is recorded, saved or sent anywhere. No accounts, servers or analytics.
